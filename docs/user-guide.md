# User Guide

For the people who *use* the platform day to day — executives, regional and DC
managers, purchasing, and finance.

## Navigating the dashboard

The left rail lists the nine pages. Start on **Executive Overview** for the
headline picture, then drill into the page matching your role:

| If you are a… | Go to | To answer |
|---|---|---|
| Executive | Executive Overview | Are revenue, margin, inventory and availability healthy? |
| Supply chain lead | Inventory Health | What's my turnover, dead stock, and days of supply? |
| Regional manager | Store Performance | Which of my stores lead or lag their region? |
| Purchasing | Supplier Analytics + Exceptions | Who's unreliable, and what do I reorder today? |
| DC manager | Warehouse Performance | What's my holding cost and capacity use? |
| Merchandising | Product Performance | What's my ABC mix and what should I exit? |

## Everyday tasks

- **Filter** — use the region/period slicers at the top; they apply across the page.
- **See detail** — right-click any bar or row → **Drillthrough** to the entity's
  detail page (e.g. a single supplier's PO history).
- **Hover** — custom tooltips show a mini-scorecard (revenue, margin, turnover,
  stock-out rate) without leaving the page.
- **Act** — the **Exceptions** page is the daily worklist: reorder-now,
  current stock-outs, overstock candidates, and late suppliers.

## What you can see (Row-Level Security)

Access is scoped to your role. Regional managers see only their region; DC
managers see only warehouses; executives see everything. You never need to
filter to "your" data — it's already limited to what you're permitted to see.

## The reports (no Power BI needed)

Even without the dashboard, three markdown reports are generated by the pipeline:

- `reports/kpi_report.md` — the headline KPIs, current numbers.
- `reports/data_quality_report.md` — whether the data passed its checks.
- `reports/eda_report.md` — exploratory charts and observations.

## Reading the KPIs

| KPI | Good direction | Rough target |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory Turnover | higher | ≥ 3.0× |
| Stock-out Rate | lower | < 2% on A-class |
| GMROI | higher | > 1.0 (below = loses money to hold) |
| Supplier OTIF | higher | ≥ 85% |
| Dead-stock % | lower | minimise |
